Change log

UI-only · No data, routing, or scoring logic changed
1

Brand alignment — kill the purple AI gradient

  • Today the assessment uses #5b5fed → #8b5cf6 (the canonical AI-slop gradient). The rest of 博创网 uses #0045c4. Two products, two brands — confusing.
  • Hero swapped to the same brand-ink (#0a1733) + brand-blue glow used on the dashboard header. Visual continuity across apps.
  • Editorial copy: monospace eyebrow ("FOUNDER · PERSONA TEST · v3") replaces the floating "✦ 创业者人格评测" pill. Feels more like a tool than a Buzzfeed quiz.
  • Headline left-aligned and reframed: "你属于哪一种创业者？" — speaks to the user's identity question directly rather than promising a feature ("3 分钟").
2

Replace "12,847 人测过" line with a stats strip

  • One tiny gray sentence ("已有 12,847 人测过") becomes a 3-column stat module: 12,847 已完成 · 23 人格类型 · 6 能力维度.
  • Numbers in font-mono tabular-nums — feels measured, not hype-y.
  • Communicates depth (23 personas, 6 dimensions) which is the actual product value, before the user has even tapped Start.
3

Emoji icons → lucide icons

  • 📖 🎯 ✨ → BookOpen / Radar / MessageSquareQuote wrapped in soft brand-blue tiles.
  • Same icon language as the dashboard (lucide is already a dep). No mixed-emoji-style problems.
  • Cards get a small mono tag in the header row (3 MIN · DATA · AI) so each card has a tone of voice consistent with the new brand.
4

Persona reveal — monogram, not emoji

  • A 6xl emoji 🚀 as your "identity badge" undermines the seriousness of a Doctor-grade product. Replaced with a typographic monogram (the first character of the persona name, here "破", on a brand-blue gradient tile).
  • Adds a small "Trailblazer · 23 类人格 · No. 04" English-bilingual line — feels like a real classification, not a meme.
  • Rarity chip moved up to a quieter amber-on-ink format, anchored to the top-right. No more "稀有 · 仅 7%" red panic-pill.
  • Keyword quote rendered in font-serif italic with a left blue rule — more "editorial portrait", less "RPG class card".
5

Radar card — bar list with mono ranks

  • Today's score list is a 2×N grid of pills with truncated descriptions — visually busy, hard to compare scores.
  • Now a single column ordered 01 → 06, with thin progress bars and right-aligned tabular numbers — instantly scannable.
  • Top-2 ranks colored brand-blue, bottom ranks fade — the "strengths" jump out without needing a colored badge.
  • Radar chart itself recolored from purple to brand-blue with vertex dots; same animation logic.
6

Quiz — light theme, segmented progress, lettered options

  • The current quiz is purple-gradient + white-rounded-bottom-sheet — feels like a guided meditation app. Hard to read 10 questions in a row on a saturated bg.
  • New quiz uses a paper-white background with a thin top brand-blue progress (10 segments, one per question, lit blue as you advance) — clearer "where am I in the journey".
  • Story prose set in a font-serif with the lead sentence and softer continuation styled differently. Reads like a short editorial scene, supports the "story-based assessment" pitch.
  • Each option gets an A/B/C/D mono badge — selected option swaps the badge to filled brand-blue with a check icon. Easier to discuss later ("I picked C") and accessible.
