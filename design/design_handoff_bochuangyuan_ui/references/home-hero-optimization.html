Change log

1

Header — from flat slab to ink lockup

  • Background changes from saturated #0045c4 slab to a deeper ink (#0a1733) with a subtle dotted texture and a soft brand-blue glow on the left — feels premium, less retina-burn.
  • Logo: replace the italic "B" letter mark with a real geometric B mark, plus a bilingual lockup that introduces the brand promise ("Doctor venture"). Sets up the "陪伴" tone immediately.
  • Adds inline product-level switcher (创业者工作台 / 官网 / 大赛入口) — useful because the codebase has 5 apps, but there's currently no way to switch between them from the header.
  • Adds global ⌘K search affordance — there's a Search icon used inside Archive but no global entry point today.
  • User block grows from avatar + chevron to avatar + name + role — uses the existing 高级合伙人 timeline data the system already has.
2

AI achievement banner — kill the slop, keep the AI

  • Removes the blue→indigo→purple blurred halo behind the card (classic AI-slop trope).
  • Removes the gradient text on 「人才大佬」(bg-clip-text bg-gradient-to-r) — uses a normal weighted heading instead.
  • Replaces the heavy rounded-[2rem] + shadow-xl shadow-blue-500/5 with a tokenized rounded-2xl + double layered shadow (1px hairline + soft cast). Calmer, more credible.
  • Adds a brand-blue left rail to subtly signal "this is an AI suggestion" without painting the whole card.
  • Tones the AI pill: from bg-blue-600 text-white shouty caps to a soft brand-blue/8 chip in normal case.
  • Two CTAs (primary + secondary) instead of one black CTA — gives users a way to dismiss politely, which matches the "accompany not nag" brand voice.
3

Progress strip — copy is now truthful

  • Current copy says "进度 8/10 再上传 2 份材料即可解锁证书 已达成 ✓" — the ✓ contradicts "再上传 2 份". Replaced with a clean fraction "8 / 10 已完成" + the heading itself ("还差 2 份材料") carrying the action.
  • Progress fill is solid brand-blue instead of from-blue-500 to-purple-500.
  • The fraction uses font-mono tabular-nums so the digits don't jitter as progress updates.
4

Competition slider — legibility & AI rationale

  • Section subtitle changes from the all-caps decorative "对接海量资源与高额奖金" to plain functional info: "本月共 12 场 · 报名中 3 场". Useful at a glance without over-narrating the AI's role.
  • Slider chrome: removes the inner blue blob blur, raises card height 220 → 240px to give the headline more air, swaps the radius from rounded-[24px] to the tokenized rounded-2xl (same visual, single source).
  • The "推荐" caps pill is replaced by a soft "报名进行中" status chip — half the visual noise.
  • Headline scales up: text-xl → text-2xl; body copy scales up text-[11px] → text-sm. The current size is below WCAG comfortable reading.
  • Replaces left-border-pipe decoration on the body copy with normal text — that pipe is one of the listed slop tropes.
5

Slider footer — urgency + bigger CTAs

  • Reward badge moves out of the image overlay and into the bottom info row, joining 截止 + 地点. Consistent metadata pattern.
  • Adds a "还剩 14 天" deadline countdown in rose — the current design only shows the absolute date, which is harder to act on.
  • CTA pairs: "查看详情" (secondary) + "立即报名" (primary). Single tiny corner button → proper two-button group on image side. Bigger hit targets.
  • Image gets a left-to-right white fade so the headline always has a clean reading band, regardless of which photo loads.
  • Carousel indicators: pairs the dots with a "01 / 03" mono counter — clearer than three dots on their own.

Cross-cutting token decisions

Radius scale

Lock to 5 tokens: 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24px (sm → 2xl) + pill. Today's home uses 9 distinct radii — most of them invisible to the eye but expensive to maintain.

Type weight semantics

Stop using font-black for non-display elements. Reserve 800 for hero headlines; use 600 (semibold) for section heads, 500 (medium) for emphasis, 400 (regular) for body. Today many tiny labels are font-black — undermines weight as a signal.

Minimum body size

Body text floor 13px (text-[13px]). Reserve 10–11px only for footnotes / mono codes / decorative all-caps eyebrows. Most of the current dashboard breaks this.
